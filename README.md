# For Praise — The Future I Want to Build With You 💍

A digital love story, interactive cinematic experience, and proposal created with deep admiration and devotion for Praise by Ernest.

## Features
- **Opening Experience**: Cinematic reveal with interactive ambient particles & graceful pauses
- **Section I — What I See In You**: Interactive unfolding cards celebrating Praise's mind, brilliance, and beauty
- **Section II — The Constellation of You**: Interactive glowing constellation map with 9 floating star qualities + hidden easter egg
- **Section III — Why You?**: Velvet burgundy cosmos transition with rotating 3D gemstone & rings
- **Section IV — A Life With You**: Visionary partnership timeline (Progress, Prayer, Adventure, Devotion, Family)
- **Section V — Imagine This With Me**: 8 interactive future scenario cards
- **Section VI — The Letter**: Handwritten parchment letter styling signed with love
- **Section VII — The Proposal**: Realistic 3D diamond engagement ring, emotional pauses, respectful branching, and screenshot-ready celebration card
- **Acoustic Soundtrack**: Real-time synthesized warm acoustic piano progression (Web Audio API)
